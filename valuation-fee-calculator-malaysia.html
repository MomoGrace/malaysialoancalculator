What is a property valuation fee in Malaysia?

A property valuation fee Malaysia is a professional charge for preparing a property valuation report Malaysia for financing, sales, purchases or portfolio planning. This valuation fee calculator Malaysia helps you estimate the valuer fee Malaysia using a tiered scale and minimum fee check.

When do you need a valuation report?

Common situations include buying a subsale home, refinancing, restructuring financing, selling house valuation checks and bank submission for loan approval. A bank valuation fee Malaysia estimate can help set realistic upfront budgeting before engagement.

Who pays valuation fees in Malaysia?

In many loan cases, the borrower or buyer usually bears the valuation report cost Malaysia, while some transactions may vary by bank package or negotiated terms. Always verify final terms in your letter of offer or appointment letter.

How are valuation fees estimated?

This house valuation fee Malaysia tool applies a tiered improved value fee scale to the property value, then enforces the minimum professional fee, and optionally adds SST and disbursement estimate to calculate total cost.

Bank valuation vs selling price

Bank valuation can differ from asking price because valuers consider comparable transactions, location, condition and market evidence. If valuation is lower than selling price, financing margin and cash outlay may change.

Valuation fee example for a RM500,000 property

For a RM500,000 property:

  • First RM100,000 × 0.25% = RM250
  • Remaining RM400,000 × 0.20% = RM800
  • Estimated professional valuation fee = RM1,050 before SST and disbursement
  • Total may be higher if SST, travel, bank or report charges apply

Valuation fee vs legal fee vs stamp duty

Valuation fee covers professional appraisal work and valuation report issuance, while legal fees cover conveyancing documentation and stamp duty is government duty on legal instruments. For legal cost planning, use our Legal Fee & Stamp Duty Calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this valuation fee calculator Malaysia exact?

No. It is an estimate for budgeting and planning only.

Does the calculator include SST automatically?

SST is optional. Enter 8 if applicable to your service engagement, or keep 0 if not applicable.

Why is there a minimum fee input?

Some jobs may have a minimum professional charge, so the calculator applies your selected minimum when the scale fee is lower.

Can I include disbursement estimate?

Yes. You can add optional disbursement estimate for courier, search, transport or report handling planning.

Can bank valuation differ from market expectation?

Yes. Bank valuation follows evidence-based assessment and may be higher or lower than listing price.

What should I confirm before appointing a valuer?

Confirm scope, report purpose, SST treatment, timeline, travel charges and final professional fee terms in writing.
