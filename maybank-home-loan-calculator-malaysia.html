How the Home Loan Repayment Formula Works

Most Malaysian home loans use a reducing balance interest method. This means interest is charged on the outstanding loan balance each month, which decreases as you repay. The formula for a flat monthly repayment is:

Monthly Repayment = P × r(1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n − 1]

Where P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12), and n is the total number of months.

What Factors Affect Your Maybank Home Loan Rate?

The interest rate offered is not a fixed universal number — it depends on several personal and property-related factors:

1. Your Credit Score and CCRIS Record

Banks in Malaysia check your CCRIS report to see how you have been managing existing debts. A clean repayment record with no defaults or late payments improves your chances of getting a lower rate. Learn more in our CCRIS report guide.

2. Your Debt Service Ratio (DSR)

Maybank and other banks use DSR to measure how much of your monthly income goes toward debt repayments. A DSR below 60% is generally considered healthy. Use the DSR calculator to check your current ratio before applying.

3. Loan Tenure

Longer tenures mean lower monthly payments but more total interest paid. The maximum home loan tenure in Malaysia is typically 35 years, subject to the borrower's age at the time of loan maturity.

4. Property Type and Location

The margin of finance offered may differ between primary market properties (new launches by developers) and secondary market properties (subsale homes). Location and property value also play a role.

How to Use the Home Loan Calculator Effectively

  1. Enter the loan amount — not the property price. If the property costs RM500,000 and you pay a 10% down payment, your loan amount is RM450,000.
  2. Use a realistic interest rate — use a realistic planning rate and test more than one scenario, because bank rates can change.
  3. Choose your intended tenure — start with 30 years and adjust downward to see how monthly repayments increase.
  4. Check affordability — your home loan repayment should generally not exceed one-third of your gross monthly income.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Forgetting to include legal fees, stamp duty, and valuation costs in your total budget
  • Assuming the calculator rate is the rate the bank will offer
  • Not checking your DSR before applying
  • Ignoring the MRTA or MLTT insurance cost that banks may bundle with home loans

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my Maybank home loan repayment?

Enter your loan amount, interest rate, and tenure into a home loan repayment calculator. For a RM400,000 loan at 4.25% over 30 years, the estimated repayment is about RM1,968 per month. Adjust the figures based on your actual loan offer.

What is the current Maybank home loan interest rate in Malaysia?

Maybank's home loan rates are tied to the Overnight Policy Rate (OPR) and vary based on borrower profile. Indicative rates for standard home loans vary by package, market conditions and borrower profile. Always confirm directly with Maybank for the latest rates.

What is the maximum home loan tenure in Malaysia?

The maximum home loan tenure is typically 35 years, subject to the condition that the loan must be fully settled by age 70. Maybank may have specific age-limit policies — confirm these when applying.

What is DSR and how does it affect my application?

DSR (Debt Service Ratio) is the percentage of your income used for debt repayments. Most Malaysian banks prefer a DSR below 60–70%. If your DSR is too high, the loan may be declined or offered at a lower amount. Use our DSR calculator to check yours.

Can I get a Maybank Islamic home financing instead?

Yes. Maybank offers Islamic home financing products under Shariah-compliant structures. The monthly instalment amount works similarly from a budgeting perspective, though the contract type differs. Check with Maybank's Islamic Banking division for available products.

What documents do I need to apply for a home loan?

Typically you will need your MyKad, 3 months' payslips, 6 months' bank statements, EPF statement, and a copy of the Sales and Purchase Agreement (SPA) or booking receipt. Self-employed applicants will need additional financial records.
