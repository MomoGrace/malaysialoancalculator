RHB Bank Home Loan Overview

RHB Bank is one of Malaysia's leading financial institutions offering home loans for both new launches and subsale properties. RHB provides conventional home loans as well as Islamic home financing options. The bank evaluates borrowers based on standard criteria used across Malaysian banks: income stability, existing debts, credit history, and property details.

Before applying, running an estimate through a home loan calculator can help you understand what size of loan is realistic for your income and current financial position.

Understanding DSR and Why It Matters for RHB Applications

DSR — Debt Service Ratio — is one of the primary filters banks use to assess whether you can afford a new loan. It is calculated as:

DSR = (Total Monthly Loan Repayments ÷ Gross Monthly Income) × 100

RHB, like most Malaysian banks, typically looks for a post-loan DSR below 60–70%. High earners may be assessed differently, but as a general rule, keeping your total debt commitments below two-thirds of your income improves your approval chances significantly.

Worked Example

Say you earn RM8,500 gross per month. You currently pay RM800 for a car loan and RM300 in credit card minimum payments. Your current DSR is 13%. Adding an RHB home loan repayment of RM1,968 brings your total commitments to RM3,068, which gives a DSR of 36.1% — comfortably within range.

What Is the Right Loan Amount to Apply For?

A common rule of thumb is that your home loan repayment should not exceed 30–35% of your gross income. At RM8,500 income, that suggests a repayment ceiling of roughly RM2,550–RM2,975 per month — which supports a loan of approximately RM500,000–RM600,000 at 4.25% over 30 years.

Other Factors in RHB's Assessment

CCRIS and Credit Standing

RHB reviews your CCRIS to see whether you have been managing existing loans responsibly. A record of on-time payments across all credit facilities strengthens your application. Read the CCRIS guide to understand what the report shows.

Employment Type

Salaried employees (especially civil servants and employees of established companies) tend to have smoother approval processes. Self-employed applicants need to demonstrate at least two years of consistent income, usually with income tax returns and business registration documents.

Property Age and Type

For older subsale properties (over 20–30 years), banks may offer lower margin of finance or have stricter criteria. Newer properties and developer launches often come with higher LTV options, sometimes with developer interest-bearing schemes during construction.

RHB Home Loan Application Checklist

Before sending an application, prepare a simple file with your latest payslips, bank statements, EPF statement, identification documents, existing loan details, and the property booking or sale and purchase information. A complete document set helps the banker estimate your eligibility more clearly and reduces back-and-forth after submission.

You should also compare several repayment scenarios instead of relying on one number. Test a lower loan amount, a shorter tenure, and a slightly higher planning rate. This gives you a safer view of affordability if rates move or if the bank approves a lower margin than expected.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RHB Bank's home loan interest rate?

RHB's home loan rates are linked to the OPR and vary by borrower profile. Indicative rates vary by package, market conditions and borrower profile. Confirm the latest rate with RHB Bank directly.

How is DSR calculated for an RHB home loan application?

DSR is total monthly loan repayments divided by gross monthly income, expressed as a percentage. RHB generally looks for DSR below 60–70% after including the new home loan repayment.

What is the maximum loan tenure for RHB home loans?

The maximum home loan tenure in Malaysia is typically 35 years, subject to the loan being fully repaid before the borrower turns 70. RHB's specific terms should be confirmed at application.

Does RHB offer Islamic home financing?

Yes. RHB offers Shariah-compliant home financing products through RHB Islamic Bank. Speak with the bank for the most current product options and profit rates.

Can I apply for an RHB home loan online?

RHB offers an online application channel for home loans. However, you will typically need to visit a branch or work with a banker for certain steps, such as property valuation and document submission. Check RHB's website for current application options.
