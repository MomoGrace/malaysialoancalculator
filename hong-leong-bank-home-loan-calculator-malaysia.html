Understanding How Your Rate Is Determined

Hong Leong Bank's home loan rates are generally linked to the Overnight Policy Rate (OPR) set by Bank Negara Malaysia. When OPR rises, variable rate loans typically see an increase in monthly payments. When OPR falls, payments may decrease.

Your specific rate depends on:

  • Your CCRIS repayment record
  • Your Debt Service Ratio (DSR)
  • Your income level and employment type
  • The type of property (landed vs strata, primary vs secondary market)
  • Loan amount and tenure

What Is a Healthy DSR for a Home Loan?

DSR — Debt Service Ratio — compares your total monthly loan repayments to your gross monthly income. Most Malaysian banks, including Hong Leong Bank, aim for a post-loan DSR below 60–70%.

Example: If your gross income is RM7,000 and your existing commitments total RM2,000 per month, your current DSR is about 28.6%. Adding a home loan repayment of RM1,722 brings your total to RM3,722, giving a DSR of 53.2% — within acceptable range for most banks.

How to Read Your CCRIS Report Before Applying

Your CCRIS report is one of the first things Hong Leong Bank will check. It shows up to 12 months of repayment history across all your credit accounts — home loans, car loans, personal loans, and credit cards.

If you have missed payments in recent months, the bank will see this. Even accounts that are now fully settled may show historical late payments. Address any unresolved issues before applying. Read our CCRIS report guide for details.

Tips for Getting Your Application Right

Check Affordability Honestly

Use our loan affordability calculator to find out the maximum loan amount your income can support. Then decide if the property you want falls within that range.

Factor in All Costs

The monthly repayment is just one part of the cost. Budget for stamp duty, legal fees, valuation fees, and mortgage insurance (MRTA or MLTT). These can add up to RM15,000–RM30,000 or more on a typical property purchase.

Avoid New Debts Before Applying

Taking out a new car loan or personal loan shortly before a home loan application will increase your DSR and reduce your chances of approval. Keep your debt level as low as possible in the months leading up to your application.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hong Leong Bank's home loan interest rate?

Rates vary by borrower profile and OPR. As of mid-2025, indicative rates are generally in the 4.0%–4.5% p.a. range for standard home loans. Confirm the latest rate directly with Hong Leong Bank.

How do I calculate my monthly repayment for a Hong Leong home loan?

Use a home loan calculator. Enter your loan amount (property price minus down payment), the estimated interest rate, and your desired tenure. The calculator gives you an estimated monthly figure.

Does Hong Leong Bank offer semi-flexi or full-flexi home loans?

Hong Leong Bank offers various home loan structures. Flexi home loans allow extra repayments to reduce interest. Confirm the available structures and their terms directly with the bank, as product features can change.

Can I refinance my existing home loan to Hong Leong Bank?

Yes, refinancing is possible subject to assessment. If your existing loan has a higher interest rate, refinancing may reduce your monthly repayment. Factor in the legal and processing fees involved when calculating the benefit.

What documents do I need to apply?

Typical requirements include MyKad, latest 3 months' payslips, 6 months' bank statements, EPF statement, and the SPA or booking form. Self-employed applicants need additional financial documentation such as income tax returns and audited accounts.
