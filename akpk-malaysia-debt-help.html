AKPK Malaysia: When to Seek Debt Help Before Applying for Another Loan

Last updated: May 2026  |  Reading time: ~7 min

Quick Answer AKPK (Agensi Kaunseling dan Pengurusan Kredit) is a Malaysian government agency that provides free financial counselling and a Debt Management Programme (DMP) to help individuals restructure their debts. If your monthly debt repayments are unmanageable, seeking AKPK's help before applying for new loans is the right step. Borrowers enrolled in AKPK's DMP will not be able to obtain new credit during the programme.
This guide is for general information only. It is not financial advice and is not affiliated with AKPK or any government agency. For personal debt advice, contact AKPK directly.

What Is AKPK?

Agensi Kaunseling dan Pengurusan Kredit — commonly known as AKPK — is an agency established by Bank Negara Malaysia in 2006. It provides free financial education, counselling, and debt restructuring services to Malaysians who are struggling with their loan repayments.

AKPK's services are funded by the financial industry and are completely free to consumers. There is no charge for financial counselling sessions or for enrolment in their Debt Management Programme.

AKPK's Main Services

1. Financial Counselling

AKPK counsellors help individuals understand their financial position, create a budget, and identify practical steps to manage debt. This service is available to anyone — you do not need to be in financial difficulty to attend a counselling session.

2. Financial Education

AKPK offers workshops, online tools, and educational materials on topics such as budgeting, credit management, and saving for retirement. These are publicly available through AKPK's website and branches.

3. Debt Management Programme (DMP)

The DMP is AKPK's flagship debt restructuring service. Under the DMP, AKPK negotiates with your participating banks on your behalf to restructure your repayments — often at reduced interest rates and extended tenures — into a single consolidated monthly payment made to AKPK, who then distributes it to your creditors.

How the Debt Management Programme Works

Here is what typically happens when you enrol in AKPK's DMP:

  1. Application and assessment — You attend a session at an AKPK branch and disclose all your income and debts. The counsellor assesses whether you qualify for the DMP.
  2. Negotiation with banks — AKPK contacts your participating creditors and negotiates revised repayment terms, which may include reduced interest rates.
  3. Single monthly payment — Once enrolled, you make one monthly payment to AKPK, which distributes the funds to your creditors according to the agreed schedule.
  4. Credit freeze — During the DMP period, you are not allowed to apply for new credit facilities. Your credit cards are cancelled.
  5. Programme completion — Upon completing the programme (which may take several years), you receive a certificate of completion, and you can start rebuilding your credit.
Important: Enrolling in AKPK's DMP is recorded in your CCRIS and CTOS report. During the programme period, banks will decline new loan applications. This is intentional — the DMP is designed to help you get out of debt, not take on more.

Warning Signs That You May Need AKPK's Help

Consider reaching out to AKPK if you are experiencing any of the following:

  • Your total monthly debt repayments exceed 60–70% of your income
  • You are regularly using a credit card to pay for basic necessities
  • You have missed two or more loan payments in the past 6 months
  • You are considering taking a personal loan to repay another loan
  • You are receiving calls from bank recovery teams or collection agencies
  • You feel anxious about your debt situation but do not know where to start

Use our DSR calculator to check whether your debt-to-income ratio has reached a problematic level.

AKPK vs Applying for Another Loan: Which Is Right for You?

A common mistake Malaysians make when overwhelmed by debt is to apply for a new personal loan to consolidate or cover existing payments. This rarely solves the underlying problem and usually makes the DSR worse, leading to rejection from banks anyway.

If your current DSR is already above 70% and you are struggling to meet repayments, AKPK's DMP may be a more sustainable path than additional borrowing. The DMP allows you to restructure what you already owe without adding new debt.

After Completing the AKPK DMP

Once you successfully complete the Debt Management Programme, you receive a completion certificate. Your CCRIS record will no longer show active DMP status, and you can begin rebuilding your credit through responsible borrowing — for example, using a low-limit credit card and paying it off in full each month.

Most banks will reassess you based on your post-DMP financial behaviour. It may take 12–24 months after completing the programme before you are in a position to apply for a major loan like a home loan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AKPK's service free?

Yes. Financial counselling and the Debt Management Programme are completely free for consumers. AKPK is funded by the financial industry, not by fees charged to borrowers.

Will enrolling in AKPK DMP affect my credit record?

Yes. DMP enrolment is recorded in your CCRIS report. During the programme, you cannot apply for new credit facilities. Once completed, the DMP status is removed and you can begin rebuilding your credit profile.

How long does the AKPK Debt Management Programme take?

The duration depends on your total debt level and the revised repayment terms negotiated with your creditors. It can range from a few years to longer periods. AKPK will provide an estimated timeline during your counselling session.

Which banks participate in the AKPK DMP?

Most major Malaysian banks participate in the DMP. However, AKPK can only negotiate with banks that are part of the programme. Debts with non-participating creditors are not covered by the DMP. Confirm with AKPK which of your creditors are included.

Can I still apply for a home loan after completing AKPK?

Yes, but it may take time. After completing the DMP, you should focus on rebuilding your credit profile through responsible borrowing over 12–24 months before attempting a major loan application. Your chances improve significantly with a clean post-DMP credit record.
