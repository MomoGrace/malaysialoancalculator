What is stamp duty in Malaysia?

Stamp duty is a government tax charged on legal instruments related to property transactions and financing. A stamp duty calculator Malaysia helps buyers set a realistic budget before signing documents.

What is MOT stamp duty?

MOT stamp duty is charged on the Memorandum of Transfer when property ownership is transferred. It is commonly a major upfront cost for subsale transactions.

What is loan agreement stamp duty?

Loan agreement stamp duty is usually 0.5% of the approved loan amount, so it increases when financing amount is higher.

What are SPA legal fees?

SPA legal fee Malaysia usually refers to solicitor fees for preparing and completing the Sale and Purchase Agreement based on a scale fee structure.

What are loan legal fees?

Loan legal fees are solicitor fees for preparing loan documentation and perfection work with the bank. This calculator estimates loan legal fees using the same scale framework for planning.

How much cash do you need before buying a house?

Most buyers should prepare for down payment plus MOT stamp duty, loan stamp duty, SPA legal fees and loan legal fees. Use this home buying cost calculator Malaysia together with a repayment estimate from our home loan monthly repayment guide and affordability checks from loan affordability guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this legal fee calculator Malaysia exact?

No. It is an estimate for planning and budgeting only.

Does this include SST and disbursements?

No. SST, disbursements and other professional charges are excluded in this estimate.

Can I override the loan amount manually?

Yes. Enter a manual loan amount if your approved financing differs from margin-based calculation.

Why is HDA estimate different from standard estimate?

Developer / HDA transactions often use discounted legal fee bands, so estimated SPA and loan legal fees may be lower.

What if my property price is above RM7,500,000?

This tool applies a negotiable 1% estimate for the amount above RM7,500,000 for planning.

What other costs should I prepare besides this calculator?

You may need valuation fees, MRTA/MLTA, bank processing fees, and maintenance or sinking fund charges depending on property type.
