Personal Loan Checklist

  • Compare total repayment across different tenure options.
  • Check processing fee, stamp duty, late payment charges and early settlement terms.
  • Do not borrow more than you can repay comfortably.
  • Use the DSR calculator before submitting an application.

Personal Loan FAQ

What affects personal loan monthly repayment?

The amount borrowed, interest rate and tenure are the main factors. Fees and insurance may also affect the total cost.

Is lower monthly payment always better?

Not always. A lower monthly payment from a longer tenure may increase total interest paid.

Can this calculator compare offers?

You can enter different rates and tenures to compare estimates, but you should confirm final terms with the lender.
